Current Market Condition Statistics

Low inventory has been the main thing holding prices up through the big jump in rates.

Inventory is now above 2019 levels

2019 is the baseline because it was the last normal market before inventory got tight.

If inventory levels rise through the spring, that will place downward pressure on prices.

The 10-year Treasury yield drives mortgage rates, and it will likely stay elevated because of inflation and the national debt.

30-year fixed mortgage rate

From 16.63% in 1981 to 2.96% in 2021. Today: 7.32%.

Annual average, 1980–2025; latest weekly rate Oct 22, 2026. Source: Freddie Mac PMMS.

Historically, today's rates aren't high. The issue is how fast they jumped from an artificially low point that many people came to see as normal. The market is adjusting very gradually.
